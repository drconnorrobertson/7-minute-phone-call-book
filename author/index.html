About the Author

Meet Dr. Connor Robertson.

Dr. Connor Robertson

Dr. Connor Robertson is an entrepreneur, author, and real estate investor based in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. He has built multiple businesses across tax advisory, real estate, media, and technology -- all grounded in the belief that direct human conversation is still the most powerful tool in business.

The 7 Minute Phone Call was born out of years of real-world experience making calls that mattered: cold outreach to potential partners, follow-ups with stalled prospects, negotiations on real estate deals, and conversations that turned strangers into long-term business relationships.

Dr. Robertson holds a doctoral degree and brings a research-driven, data-backed approach to everything he publishes. His work sits at the intersection of sales psychology, behavioral economics, and practical entrepreneurship.

When he is not writing or building businesses, you can find him analyzing real estate deals, recording content, or picking up the phone and making the next 7-minute call.
