Call vs Email Follow-Up for a Stalled Proposal

A stalled proposal does not automatically need another call or a longer email. Choose the channel by the uncertainty you need to resolve and the buyer's preferred way to communicate. The purpose of a follow-up is to clarify a decision, including a decision to stop, rather than to create activity for its own sake.

7 Responses to “Send Me Information” in Sales

When a prospect says send me information, they may want a useful resource, need time or prefer to end the conversation. Do not assume the request is either a buying signal or a disguised objection. One brief clarifying question can help you respond appropriately without turning a simple request into an interrogation.

7 Checks Before Ending a Sales Discovery Call

A discovery call is useful when both participants leave with a clearer view of the problem and what happens next. An automatic demo booking is not always the right outcome. Use these seven checks to decide whether another conversation, a specific answer or an honest close is appropriate.
