About the Book

What it is, who it is for, and what you will get from reading it.

The Problem Every Entrepreneur Faces

You had a great first conversation. The prospect was engaged, asked smart questions, and seemed ready to move forward. Then... silence. No reply to your follow-up. No returned calls. The deal that felt like a sure thing just evaporated.

This happens to every entrepreneur, every sales professional, every investor. Conversations stall. Leads go cold. Deals die on the vine -- not because the fit was wrong, but because nobody knew how to restart the conversation.

The 7 Minute Phone Call was written to solve this exact problem. It provides a repeatable, structured framework for picking up the phone and turning stalled conversations into forward momentum -- in seven minutes or less.

What You Will Learn

  • The 4-step framework (Hook, Problem, Bridge, Close) that works in any industry
  • Why seven minutes is the optimal call length backed by psychology and data
  • Word-for-word scripts you can use on your very next call
  • How to handle objections without being pushy or manipulative
  • The mindset shift that turns phone anxiety into phone confidence
  • How to build a sustainable calling habit that generates consistent deal flow
  • Advanced techniques for high-stakes negotiations and large deals

Results Readers Get

Readers who apply the 7-minute framework consistently report:

  • Restarting conversations with leads that had gone silent for weeks or months
