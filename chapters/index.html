Chapter Overviews

10 chapters that take you from phone anxiety to phone mastery.

  1. Why Seven Minutes Changes Everything

    The science behind short, structured calls. Why longer calls lose deals and shorter ones build trust. Data-backed evidence that seven minutes is the sweet spot for moving any conversation forward.

  2. The Mindset Before the Dial

    How to overcome call reluctance and phone anxiety. The mental framework that separates confident callers from nervous ones. Practical exercises to rewire your relationship with the phone.

  3. The Hook: Earning the First 30 Seconds

    Pattern interrupts that work. How to open a call so the person on the other end actually wants to keep talking. The three types of hooks and when to use each one.

  4. The Problem: Surfacing Real Pain

    Asking questions that reveal what your prospect actually needs, not what they say they need. The psychology of pain points and how to guide someone to articulate their own problem.

  5. The Bridge: Connecting Without Pitching

    How to position your solution as the natural answer without sounding like a salesperson. The art of the conversational bridge that feels like advice, not a sales pitch.

  6. The Close: Next Steps That Stick

    Why most calls fail at the close and how to make yours different. Specific closing techniques that create commitment, not just agreement. Calendar-first closing strategies.

  7. Handling Objections Like a Pro

    The five objections you will hear on every call and exactly how to respond to each one. Framework for turning objections into opportunities without being pushy or confrontational.

  8. Restarting Stalled Conversations

    The specific playbook for reaching back out to leads that went silent. Timing, messaging, and tone strategies for re-engagement calls. How to reference the past without sounding desperate.

  9. Building a Calling System

    How to build a sustainable calling habit that generates consistent deal flow. Time blocking, CRM integration, tracking metrics, and creating accountability. The daily, weekly, and monthly cadence that top performers use.

  10. Advanced Plays: High-Stakes and Enterprise Calls

    Adapting the 7-minute framework for larger deals, multiple stakeholders, and complex negotiations. Strategies for executive-level conversations and multi-call sequences that build toward major closes.
