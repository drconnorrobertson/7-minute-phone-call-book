What is The 7 Minute Phone Call about?

The book focuses on restarting stalled conversations and moving business discussions forward through short, structured calls. Its companion resources cover preparation, clear questions, recaps and follow-through. AI guides support preparation and documentation rather than replacing the human conversation.

How do you restart a stalled business conversation?

Refer to the last meaningful exchange, ask whether the subject remains relevant and invite an update. Do not assume silence tells you why the conversation paused. A useful opening creates room for a new timeline, a changed requirement or a clear decision.

How can I follow up without being pushy?

Reconnect around a real commitment or useful question. Make the message easy to answer and respect communication preferences. Avoid manufactured urgency. A well-timed call should help clarify the next decision, including whether to defer the conversation.

What belongs in a follow-up call agenda?

Include a short opening, the current status, one or two clarifying questions and a proposed next step. Prepare from the actual history. Let the conversation change the agenda when the other person introduces information you did not have.

What should I say when a prospect stops responding?

Ask whether the topic is still a priority and whether there is anything needed to decide. Keep the wording respectful and brief. Do not turn an unanswered message into a claim about the contact interest or intent.

How do you follow up with a business seller?

Return to the agreed stage of the acquisition discussion. Confirm whether requested records are available, whether the timeline has changed and who owns the next action. Preserve confidentiality and avoid implying financing or purchase commitments that have not been approved.

How do you end a call with a clear next step?

Summarize the decision, name the responsible person and confirm the date or trigger for the next action. Ask whether that summary reflects the conversation. If no action was agreed, record the open question rather than inventing a deadline.

How can AI help prepare a call?

Give it authorized notes and messages. Ask for the last confirmed status, outstanding commitments and unanswered questions, with source references. Review the brief before calling. The tool should organize known information without guessing personal motives.

What should a business call recap include?

Separate confirmed decisions, assigned actions and open questions. Include only agreed dates and responsibilities. Review the draft before sending, then store the final version beside the next task so the team can follow through.

How should a follow-up system measure progress?

Track fulfilled commitments, information exchanged and agreed next steps. Message volume alone does not establish relationship progress. Organize the system around useful actions and communication preferences rather than sending the same reminder indefinitely.
