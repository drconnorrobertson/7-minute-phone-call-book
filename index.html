The seven-minute operating system

A call is a decision path, not a performance

The strongest prospecting conversations are short because they are designed around one useful outcome: determine whether a real problem, a credible fit, and a sensible next step exist. This field guide expands the four-part framework into a repeatable practice for founders, sales teams, investors, and advisors who need to earn attention without wasting it.

Questions readers ask before they act

What makes a seven-minute call successful?

Clarity. Both people should leave knowing whether the problem matters, whether there may be a fit, and what happens next.

Should every call be exactly seven minutes?

No. Seven minutes is a discipline for reaching a decision point quickly, not a timer that cuts off a useful conversation.

How much scripting is enough?

Script the opening, the key diagnostic questions, and the close. Keep the middle responsive to what the prospect actually says.

What if the prospect says “send information”?

Ask what information would help them decide and agree on when you will reconnect; otherwise the request is usually a polite exit.

How do managers coach the framework?

Score observable behaviors-context, questions, listening, summary, and next step-rather than judging personality or charisma.

When should a rep disqualify?

When the problem is not meaningful, the timing is wrong, access to a decision is impossible, or the offer cannot responsibly help.
